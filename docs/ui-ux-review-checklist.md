# UI/UX Review Checklist

## Journey Review

- Candidate onboarding, profile, verification, job search, application, learning,
  and messages flows are complete and understandable.
- Employer onboarding, verification, job posting, applications, billing, and
  dashboard flows are complete and understandable.
- Empty states are honest, actionable, and do not show fake proof.

## Visual Quality

- Buttons are visible and use clear labels or familiar icons.
- Contrast meets accessible standards.
- Text fits within containers on mobile and desktop.
- Forms show validation and success/error states.
- Layouts remain stable during loading and after data changes.

## Accessibility

- Pages have clear headings.
- Form fields have labels.
- Interactive controls are keyboard reachable.
- Focus states are visible.
- Mobile navigation remains usable.

## AfriTalent-Specific Checks

- Trust and scam guidance is visible where candidates may be contacted.
- Employer premium branding is clearly gated.
- Application paths distinguish AfriTalent direct apply from employer-site apply.
- Learning course progress reflects persisted state.
