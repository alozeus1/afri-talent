# AfriTalent Product Context

AfriTalent connects African talent with global and remote opportunities through
trust-first hiring workflows, candidate readiness tools, employer verification,
and AI-assisted application support.

## Current Product Priorities

- Reliable job discovery, search relevance, and application flows.
- Candidate dashboard stability and profile completeness.
- Employer onboarding, posting, verification, and candidate review workflows.
- Trust and scam-prevention guidance across messaging and applications.
- Learning progress that persists beyond local browser state.
- Premium employer features, including candidate visibility and branding.
- Mara AI context over jobs, applications, and platform state.

## User Groups

- Candidates seeking global, remote, visa-friendly, or relocation-friendly roles.
- Employers posting roles, verifying trust, reviewing applicants, and managing
  hiring workflow.
- Admins reviewing verification, abuse reports, trust cases, and platform health.

## Product Guardrails

- Do not fake employer verification, testimonials, salaries, or government
  immigration content.
- Do not expose proprietary ranking logic, trust-scoring details, or unpublished
  roadmap specifics in public-facing copy.
- AI features must use available product data and clearly avoid fabricating
  candidate, employer, or job facts.
- Verification automation may prepare findings, but final sensitive approvals
  require human review unless an explicitly approved safe rule exists.
