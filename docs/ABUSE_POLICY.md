# ABUSE POLICY

## Purpose
AfriTalent exists to enable credible cross-border hiring. We remove or limit behavior that undermines authenticity, safety, or trust.

## Prohibited behavior
- fake employers or fake candidate identities
- impersonation of known companies or real people
- requests for application, processing, equipment, or training fees
- attempts to force users off-platform immediately for opaque communication
- misleading salary bait or intentionally deceptive job information
- spam applications, spam outreach, or automated low-quality submissions
- abusive, harassing, or coercive communication

## Enforcement principles
- Enforcement is evidence-based, not badge- or report-count-only.
- High-severity signals can trigger immediate holds.
- Lower-confidence cases should be reviewed by admins before severe action.
- Every moderation action must include a reason code and action history.

## Enforcement actions
- content warning
- job hold for review
- application hold for review
- verification request
- account limitation
- account suspension
- reinstatement after review

## Evidence standards
- Badges require platform-backed checks, not self-attestation.
- Business-doc approval requires readable supporting evidence.
- Candidate evidence should map clearly to the claimed identity, skill, or employment claim.
- A single report should not automatically create a severe sanction unless the evidence is explicit.

## Appeal and reinstatement
- Suspended or limited users should be able to provide additional evidence.
- Reinstatement decisions must be written into case history.
- Repeat abuse after reinstatement should trigger enhanced scrutiny and stronger enforcement.

## User safety guidance
- Never pay to get access to a job.
- Be cautious with urgent pressure to move to WhatsApp, Telegram, or private email.
- Report suspicious jobs, profiles, or messages from the trust center or directly from the product surfaces.
