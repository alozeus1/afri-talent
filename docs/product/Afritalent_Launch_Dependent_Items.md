# AfriTalent Launch-Dependent Items

Date: 2026-04-28

## Can Implement Now

- Accessibility fixes such as skip links, focus-visible styles, keyboard-friendly controls, aria labels, and reduced-motion handling.
- Hover, active, disabled, loading, skeleton, and empty-state polish.
- Honest early-access messaging.
- Learning content and practice interview scenarios.
- Rule-based recommended next actions.
- Local or lightweight feedback capture.
- Trust education, scam warnings, source transparency, and cautious labels.
- Sample salary guidance if clearly labeled as market guidance, not AfriTalent outcome data.

## Requires Real Launch Data

- Real testimonials.
- Real candidate placement outcomes.
- Real employer partnership claims.
- Real employer reviews.
- Real salary reports from AfriTalent users.
- Real success metrics such as hires, salary increases, application success rates, or interview conversion.
- Public ratings from G2, Capterra, Product Hunt, app stores, or similar channels.
- Verified case studies.

## Product Rule

If the platform cannot verify a claim from production data, user permission, or documented operational review, the UI should label it as sample, beta, market guidance, practice scenario, or coming soon.

## Current Implementation

- Homepage testimonials have been replaced with an early-access proof block.
- Fallback homepage stats now show product-readiness signals instead of fake user/employer counts.
- Salary guidance uses explicit sample-market labeling.
- Interview examples are labeled as sample scenarios and use company type instead of fake company names.
