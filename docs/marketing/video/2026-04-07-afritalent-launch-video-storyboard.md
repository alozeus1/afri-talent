# AfriTalent Launch Video Storyboard

Date: April 7, 2026

Figma board:
- https://www.figma.com/design/YzkZdEQPbeE9th1Lxkg1GY

## Goal

Create a premium short launch film for AfriTalent that positions the product as:

- trust-first
- Africa-to-global
- cleaner and more credible than a generic job board
- visually refined enough to support a high-end product launch

## Audience

- founders and hiring managers
- recruiters and talent teams
- ambitious African professionals
- operator-minded builders and partners

## Runtime

- Primary cut: 45 seconds
- Short cut: 15 seconds
- Format: 16:9 master, then crop/retime for LinkedIn and X

## Narrative Arc

### Act 1: The problem

Visual:
- crowded hiring interfaces
- noisy application lists
- generic profiles and low-confidence signals

Message:
- too much hiring noise
- too little trust
- too many strong candidates still undersold

### Act 2: The shift

Visual:
- AfriTalent interface emerges from blur into glossy focus
- trust badges, role cards, and search signals resolve cleanly
- candidate and employer workflow feels premium and calm

Message:
- AfriTalent is built around signal, trust, and workflow

### Act 3: The product edge

Visual:
- trusted role cards
- candidate AI workflow
- employer onboarding and analytics
- cross-border readiness cues

Message:
- smaller shortlists
- stronger trust
- more credible Africa-to-global hiring

### Act 4: The close

Visual:
- clean hero lockup
- confident UI motion
- logo and thesis line

Message:
- AfriTalent
- trust-first hiring for Africa-to-global opportunity

## Visual Direction

- glossy interface reflections
- rich emerald and deep ink palette
- warm amber highlights to imply opportunity and momentum
- precise typography, not generic startup softness
- slow confident camera moves rather than flashy edits

## On-Screen Copy

### 45-second version

1. Hiring is full of noise.
2. Great talent still gets overlooked.
3. Trust should be visible.
4. Workflow should feel sharper.
5. Opportunity should travel farther.
6. AfriTalent
7. Trust-first Africa-to-global hiring.

### 15-second version

1. Less hiring noise.
2. More trust.
3. Better Africa-to-global matching.
4. AfriTalent

## Sound Direction

- low, cinematic pulse
- restrained percussion
- glassy transition accents
- no hype-trailer energy
- closer to premium product film than ad montage

## Asset Plan

- Sora clips for abstract cinematic beats
- Figma-derived UI boards for interface motion references
- Remotion composition for timing, typography, end card, and assembly

## Production Notes

- keep text short and legible
- avoid overclaiming scale or outcomes not yet proven
- prioritize trust, workflow, and credibility over generic AI language
- use UI closeups and macro motion, not only abstract visuals
