# TRUST & SAFETY METRICS

## North-star outcomes
- Employer confidence in candidate authenticity
- Candidate confidence in job legitimacy
- Lower scam exposure without killing conversion

## Core metrics

### Trust coverage
- % of employers by verification tier
- % of candidates by verification tier
- % of published jobs with reviewed employer badge
- % of talent search results with premium-filter-eligible trust state

### Fraud prevention
- scam reports per 1,000 active users
- confirmed scam cases per 1,000 jobs
- % of harmful jobs blocked before publication
- % of harmful messages blocked before delivery
- duplicate employer accounts caught before publishing
- high-velocity application bursts auto-held

### Moderation operations
- verification review SLA
- report triage SLA
- case resolution SLA
- queue backlog by type
- % of cases escalated
- suspension reversal rate

### Model quality
- false-positive rate on held jobs
- false-positive rate on held applications
- false-positive rate on blocked messages
- false-negative rate estimated from confirmed post-publication scams
- appeal success rate by action type

### Business and UX impact
- employer onboarding completion after trust prompts
- job posting conversion before and after trust gate
- candidate verification completion rate
- candidate apply conversion after trust UI additions
- premium adoption tied to verified-candidate filters
- repeat employer posting rate for verified vs unverified employers

## Recommended dashboards
- executive trust health dashboard
- moderation operations dashboard
- employer verification funnel
- candidate verification funnel
- blocked content and false-positive review dashboard

## Alert thresholds
- sudden spike in scam reports
- sudden spike in held jobs
- sudden spike in blocked messages
- OTP verification failure rate spike
- moderation backlog outside SLA
