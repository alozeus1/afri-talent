# Product Marketing Context

*Last updated: April 7, 2026*

## Product Overview
**One-liner:** AfriTalent is an Africa-to-global talent platform designed to help qualified African professionals access stronger opportunities while giving employers more trust, verification, and workflow depth than a generic job board.

**What it does:** AfriTalent combines job discovery, employer workflows, candidate application tooling, trust signals, and early AI-assisted features into a single hiring platform. The product is being shaped to improve the quality of matches, reduce recruiter uncertainty, and help talent present themselves more competitively for global roles.

**Product category:** Talent marketplace, recruiting platform, employer hiring workflow

**Product type:** Two-sided B2B2C platform

**Business model:** Employer-led monetization through recruiting workflow, premium hiring tooling, and platform services; candidate-side premium services may follow over time

## Target Audience
**Target companies:** Startups, SMBs, growth companies, and globally distributed teams hiring technical talent

**Decision-makers:** Founders, hiring managers, talent leads, recruiting teams, operations leaders

**Primary use case:** Help employers discover and engage stronger African talent with more confidence, while helping candidates access better global opportunities

**Jobs to be done:**
- Find credible, high-signal talent faster
- Help candidates package themselves better for competitive roles
- Create a more trusted hiring flow than a simple listing board

**Use cases:**
- Employer hiring for technical and digital roles
- Candidate application and profile improvement
- Trust-first screening and moderation
- Future AI-assisted recruiter workflow

## Personas
| Persona | Cares about | Challenge | Value we promise |
|---------|-------------|-----------|------------------|
| Founder / Hiring Lead | Speed, signal quality, trust | Too much noise and too little confidence in applicants | Better candidate trust, workflow, and shortlist quality |
| Recruiter / Talent Ops | Workflow efficiency, candidate quality, explainability | Manual screening is slow and hard to scale | More structured workflow and stronger decision support |
| Candidate | Access, credibility, differentiation | Great talent is often undersold or filtered out | Better visibility, stronger profile signaling, and smarter application support |

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Generic job boards create too much noise and too little confidence for both employers and candidates.

**Why alternatives fall short:**
- They optimize for listings volume rather than hiring quality
- They offer weak trust signals and limited workflow depth
- They rarely serve Africa-to-global hiring needs intentionally

**What it costs them:** Slower hiring, lower confidence, missed opportunities, weaker candidate conversion, and wasted recruiter time

**Emotional tension:** Uncertainty, skepticism, frustration, and the feeling that strong candidates and strong roles are still not finding each other efficiently

## Competitive Landscape
**Direct:** Africa-focused job boards and hiring platforms that focus on listings, CV search, or basic employer tools

**Secondary:** Global recruiting platforms, recruiting agencies, ATS products, and LinkedIn-based sourcing workflows

**Indirect:** Informal referrals, WhatsApp groups, founder networks, and manual sourcing

## Differentiation
**Key differentiators:**
- Trust and verification as a first-class product layer
- Better employer workflow direction than a simple board
- AI-assisted candidate and recruiter workflow foundation
- Intentional Africa-to-global positioning

**How we do it differently:** AfriTalent is being designed around trust, workflow depth, and future intelligence layers rather than just listings and applications.

**Why that's better:** Better trust reduces screening friction. Better workflow improves hiring efficiency. Better intelligence should improve match quality over time.

**Why customers choose us:** They want a more modern, higher-signal, and more intentional hiring experience than legacy board-style alternatives.

## Objections
| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "Why not just use LinkedIn?" | LinkedIn is broad, but AfriTalent is being built for higher-signal Africa-to-global matching, trust, and workflow depth. |
| "Is this just another job board?" | No. The direction is trust-first workflow and intelligence-assisted hiring, not a thin listings marketplace. |
| "Is it ready for broad rollout?" | It is in serious pre-prod shaping with a working cloud delivery path and a focused reliability hardening phase. |

**Anti-persona:** Teams looking only for the cheapest listings channel or users who do not value trust, workflow quality, or structured hiring

## Switching Dynamics
**Push:** Frustration with noise, slow screening, low confidence, and fragmented tooling

**Pull:** A stronger trust layer, better workflow, and a clearer Africa-to-global talent thesis

**Habit:** Existing recruiter habits around LinkedIn, agencies, spreadsheets, and legacy job boards

**Anxiety:** Concern about platform maturity, candidate volume, and switching effort
